Running models on your own machine

Hosted models are convenient, but every request leaves your browser. For notes, drafts and internal documents, that trade-off is often not worth it.

Local models keep sensitive text on your own machine. They are smaller than hosted ones, but a task-specific model often closes the gap.

WHY IT MATTERS

You can pause, switch models, or go back to the original page at any time. Nothing is written to the page unless you ask for it.

Translation is only one way to work with a page. The reading assistant can summarise it, and the on-page agent can fill a form — both with your explicit approval.

WHAT STAYS LOCAL

Settings, the current translation session and chat history live in browser storage on this machine. There is no account, no sync, and no telemetry.

You bring the model: any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or a local Ollama instance. Keys are read only by the extension's background worker.
